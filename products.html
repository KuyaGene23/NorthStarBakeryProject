    The Signature Loaf

    Breads

    Baked every morning.

    $8 to $12 per loaf

    • Signature Loaf Country sourdough with a crisp crust and an open, chewy crumb.
    • Honey Oat Sandwich Bread (employee favorite) Soft, lightly sweet, and sized for lunchbox sandwiches.
    • Seeded Rye Dense and hearty, made with caraway and sunflower seeds.

    Pastries

    Ready when the doors open.

    $3 to $6 each

    • Butter Croissant Laminated over two days with local butter.
    • Cinnamon Roll (employee favorite) Soft, generously spiced, and finished with cream cheese icing.
    • Seasonal Fruit Danish Filled with the fruit choices of strawberry, blueberries, or raspberries, depending on the season.
    • Oatmeal Creme Cookies (employee favorite) Oatmeal, coconut creme filling, and butter swirl, sold singly or by the dozen.

    Cakes

    Made to order with at least 3 days of notice.

    $36 to $100, depending on size and decoration

    • Celebration Seven Layer Cake Vanilla, chocolate, or strawberry, with your choice of buttercream.
    • Coconut Creme Cake A family recipe with walnuts, shredded coconut, and cream cheese frosting.
    • Custom Event Sheet Cake Serves 24 to 48 guests for school, parties, and community events.

    Planning an event? Tell us the date and guest count on the Contact and Pre-Orders page and we will help you choose.
