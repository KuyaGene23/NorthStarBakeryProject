Welcome to North Star Bakery

We are a small neighborhood bakery on North Star Lane. Everything in the case is mixed, shaped, and baked by hand each morning, and we love knowing our customers by name. Stop in on your way to work, bring the kids on Saturdays for 10% off, or let us bake for your next gathering.

Our Specialties

  • Breads Slow-fermented sourdough and sandwich loaves, baked fresh every morning.
  • Pastries Butter croissants, cinnamon rolls, variety of delicious cookies, seasonal fruit danishes, etc.
  • Cakes custom-made for birthdays, weddings, and community events.
  • Fast Counter Service Friendly and efficient help that gets commuters out the door quickly.
