About North Star Bakery

One storefront, one oven, and the same passion of working since 2016.

Our Story

North Star Bakery opened in 2016, when US Navy Veteran, Barret Wallace started selling sourdough from a folding table at the Saturday farmers market. Neighbors kept asking where they could buy it during the week, so he rented a small storefront on North Star Lane and filled the front window with bread.

A decade later, we are still in the same spot and still baking the same way: by hand, in small batches, for the people who live and work around us.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

Good bread starts with good ingredients, and we would rather buy them from people we know.

  • Flour is stone-milled from grain grown by farms within 150 miles.
  • Butter, milk, and eggs come from local dairies and family farms.
  • Fruit fillings follow the season, so the danish changes with the market.
  • Unsold bread is donated to the neighborhood food shelf every evening.

Meet the Staff
